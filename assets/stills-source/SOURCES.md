# Stills: raw sources

Raw frames for the stills pipeline (`scripts/stills.mjs`). They are not graded or resized here. Letterbox bars, fan-channel banners and player overlays have been cropped off; nothing else has been touched. The film file, or a DVD or Blu-ray of each source film, remains the preferred source (spec §7, decision 1). These are interim frames from public fan projects and from YouTube thumbnails of scene uploads.

Credit: « La Classe Américaine — Le Grand Détournement (1993). © Warner Bros., Canal+. »

**Timecodes and source films.** LCA times and source-film attributions come from cyclim.se's shot table (https://cyclim.se/fullscript.html). It maps all 872 shots to the source film and to that film's own timecode. A frame matched to a scene by eye, without its exact shot, is marked ◌.

Notes:

- `welles.jpg`: a small player timestamp sat in the top-left corner and was cropped off with a 24 px strip.
- `steven-card.jpg` keeps its original pixels, because `stills.data.ts` pre-crops it by rectangle. `steven-closeup.jpg` is the sharper alternative.
- The YouTube files are thumbnails: about 1280 px at best, often 480 to 640 px, with re-encoding artefacts.
- Still to find: le patron du journal (Jason Robards, *All the President's Men*), and a hero-grade George.
- The news is a radio bulletin (« La radio », 0:03:26 to 0:03:41) heard over a car chase (*Harper*, *Freebie and the Bean*). There is no newsreel footage to grab; the « Actualités » card is ours to design.
- The « Attention ! » warning is a voice-over (« Première voix off », read by Raymond Loyer per French Wikipedia) between fades to black at 0:00:03; set it as type.
